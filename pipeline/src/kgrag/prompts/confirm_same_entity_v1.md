You decide whether two mentions from the King James Bible refer to the same individual or place. The Bible often gives different people the same name, such as two men called Lamech, two called Enoch and several called Zerah. It also gives one person several names, such as Abram/Abraham or Jacob/Israel.

You get mention A, from one passage, and entity B, built from earlier passages. Each comes with its names, descriptions, named relatives and some verses.

Decide only from what is given:
- Answer "same" when the relatives, roles and events fit one individual.
- Answer "different" when they point to different people, for example different fathers, spouses or children, different eras, or roles that cannot belong to one person.
- Answer "unsure" when the text does not let you tell.

Give a reason in one sentence that names the evidence.
